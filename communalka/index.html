Communalka for iPhone: Privacy Policy

Communalka: Party Card Game for iPhone (org.bigmeco.communalka) is developed by Bigmeco / Nikolai Ilin. This section covers the iPhone release with advertising and without in-app purchases. Contact: bigmecoprog@gmail.com.

Local settings and game sessions

No game account is required. Your chosen nickname, interface language and sound settings are stored on your device. Active match information is processed by the participating devices during the session. Bigmeco does not operate a game server, cloud profiles or a separate developer analytics or automatic crash-reporting service. Local match identifiers may be retained to prevent repeated full-screen ads.

Nearby multiplayer

Nearby Connections uses Bluetooth and Wi-Fi to find and connect devices in the same physical area. iOS may request Bluetooth and Local Network access. The game does not request GPS coordinates. Playing alone with bots does not require nearby-device discovery.

During discovery, nearby players can see your apartment name and host nickname. After both players verify the matching connection code, devices exchange player and session identifiers, nicknames, lobby information, moves and game state over an encrypted Nearby connection. Discovery information is visible before that verification. Choose a nickname and room name you are comfortable sharing. There is no voice or text chat. Leaving a game does not remove information already received by the other participants.

Google supplies the Nearby SDK; any technical information it processes is subject to Google's policies. Android participants may also use Google Play services and its usage and diagnostics settings, as described in the Android sections below.

Advertising and your choices

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob supplies labelled native ads and full-screen ads after completed games. The game does not deliberately send player nicknames, room names, connection codes, cards or game moves to the advertising service.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, advertising data, app and ad interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics. Google and its advertising partners use this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention under their own policies. SDK transfers are encrypted in transit. Non-personalised or limited ads can still involve data processing.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent and privacy choices. Advertising requests depend on whether the SDK permits them. Where applicable, the ad privacy option in the game lets you revisit your choices. If iOS asks for permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites, you can refuse and continue playing. You can manage that permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Consent choices and Apple's tracking permission serve different purposes.

Google Mobile Ads data disclosure · Google UMP · Google Privacy Policy

Purchases

This iPhone release has no in-app purchases or ad-removal purchase. Google Play purchases described below apply to Android and do not grant an iPhone entitlement.

Retention, deletion and requests

Local settings remain until replaced or deleted. To remove local app data, delete the app rather than only offloading it. Temporary game-session data is discarded when the session ends. Deleting your app does not delete copies on other players' devices or records retained by Google, its advertising partners or Apple under their policies.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the details you provide and use them to answer your request. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve it and meet applicable legal obligations. Write to bigmecoprog@gmail.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information Bigmeco holds. Use the providers' privacy controls for information they hold.

Age suitability and this website

The game includes references to alcohol, tobacco and adult domestic themes. Check the age rating shown by the App Store for your country or region. It is not designed as a children's app.

This policy page has no advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical visit information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We update this policy and its effective date when our practices change.

Privacy Policy for Communalka

Communalka: Party Card Game for Android (org.bigmeco.communalka) is developed by Bigmeco / Nikolai Ilin. For privacy questions or requests, write to bigmecoprog@gmail.com.

On your device

You can play without a game account. The app stores your chosen nickname, language, sound settings and a limited record of recent match identifiers used to avoid repeating full-screen ads. These settings stay on your device. Active match data is held by the participating devices while the game session runs. Bigmeco does not maintain a server or cloud profiles for the game.

Playing with people nearby

When you host a match, nearby players searching for a game can see the name of your apartment and your host nickname. After both players verify the connection code, their devices exchange nicknames, lobby information, moves and game state over an encrypted Google Nearby Connections link. Use a nickname and a room name you are comfortable sharing with people nearby. The game has no voice or text chat.

Android may ask for Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi or local network access, or location permission on older versions. The game uses these permissions to find and connect to nearby players and does not read your GPS coordinates. You can manage the permissions in Android settings. Playing with bots does not require Nearby permissions.

Google Play services may collect Nearby connection analytics, including discovery latency, reliability and throughput, along with device model, country, build version and the app package name. Google uses this information to improve Nearby. Device usage and diagnostics controls may let you manage that collection.

Google Nearby Connections and data collection

Ads and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob displays ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location, app interactions such as launches and ad taps, performance diagnostics, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, support analytics and prevent fraud. Google encrypts data collected by the SDK in transit.

Google’s User Messaging Platform handles ad privacy choices and consent where applicable. If it asks you to review your choices, you can revisit them from the ad privacy option in the game settings when that option is available. You can manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings where supported. The one-time Remove Ads purchase disables both advertising formats in the game.

Purchases and restoration

Google Play processes payment for Remove Ads. The app receives purchase information from Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase status and a signed receipt with a purchase token. Google Play keeps the transaction and determines its retention under Google’s policies. Clearing local game data does not delete that transaction.

Temporary game session data is discarded when the session ends. You can restore an eligible purchase through Google Play after reinstalling the app.
